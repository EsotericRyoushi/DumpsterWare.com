Games / Red Sands

Working title · In development

No heroes left.
Just a life to live.

Red Sands is a life-sim sandbox on a partially colonized Mars, long after the old order collapsed. You're the descendant of a bloodline the world has outgrown — make a life while the colonies, factions, and a distant Earth fight over what comes next.

The idea

A life in the wreckage of someone else's story.

Mars was supposed to be the next start. Then the old order collapsed, and the colonies learned to keep going without it. The cities survived, the frontier spread, and contact with Earth narrowed to trade — trade that still runs on Earth's terms.

You're the descendant of a bloodline that mattered once, back when there were still heroes and enemies worth naming. That world is gone. What's left is a life to build, factions deciding what Mars becomes, and a lot of people who'd rather you picked a side.

You can live in the city and get caught in the politics, or move to a small colony and scrape by. Either way, the story happens around you — you just have to live through it.

The loop

Build a life while the world makes up its mind.

Three ideas we keep coming back to while the systems take shape.

01

Build a life

Find a trade, a roof, and a routine. Work the job, learn the town, and make something that's yours.

02

Live with factions

The cities and colonies have their own politics. Pick a side, stay neutral, or leave before it finds you — but the world keeps moving without your permission.

03

Keep going

No credits, no final boss, no correct way to play. Five minutes or five hours — the world should be happy to see you either way.

What we're building toward

A world that doesn't wait for you.

A working list, not a promise list. It will change as the game teaches us what it wants to be.

In development

Design goals

A Mars with its own politics

The cities, colonies, and factions should have their own agendas. Trade with Earth, local grudges, and the question of who gets to decide what Mars becomes — all of it should keep moving whether you're watching or not.

  • Cities and colonies with their own politics
  • Factions that act without asking you
  • A trade loop that grounds the day-to-day
  • A frontier you can disappear into
  • Saves that respect your time
  • Life sim
  • Sandbox
  • Post-collapse

On purpose

What's not in it.

Just as important as the feature list.

01

No chosen one

You're the descendant of an old bloodline, not a prophecy. The world doesn't owe you a destiny, and nobody's waiting for you to save it.

02

No correct side

The factions aren't good and evil. Back one, stay out of it, or leave for the frontier. The world will have opinions either way.

03

No finish line

No credits, no final boss, no end-game screen. The point is the life you make while the rest of Mars is busy deciding what it wants to be.
