The company

Small studio. Long memory.

DumpsterWare started in 2021 as an excuse to make games. It grew into a place to build the tools we wanted and the stories we couldn't find anywhere else.

We're independent, self-funded, and allergic to filler. Whether it's a conduit calculator or a narrative game, the rule is the same — if it wouldn't survive a jobsite or a bad internet connection, it isn't finished.

— Hunter, DumpsterWare

How we work

Three things we won't ship without.

The standards don't change when the medium does. A game, an app, and a calculator all get measured the same way.

01

Good bones

Built on boring, dependable technology. No trend tax, no rewrite every spring, no house of cards behind the pretty part.

02

No filler

Every screen has a job. If it doesn't help you do the thing, understand the thing, or enjoy the thing, it doesn't make the cut.

03

Built to last

Offline-first where it counts, account-free where we can manage it, and readable on a phone in the sun with gloves on.
