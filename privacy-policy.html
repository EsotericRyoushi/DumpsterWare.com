Privacy Policy

EMT Bender Buddy · Effective November 19, 2026

EMT Bender Buddy is an offline conduit-bending calculator developed by DumpsterWare (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what the app does with information on your device.

We do not collect any data

The app collects no personal information and no sensitive information. It does not ask you to create an account, does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or any device identifier, and does not send anything from your device to us or to anyone else.

There are no analytics services, advertising networks, crash-reporting services, or other third-party software development kits in the app. The app contains no ads and no trackers.

What stays on your device

The measurements and settings you enter are used only to calculate bend marks and cut lengths on your device. To let the app resume where you left off, it saves that calculator state to a local file inside the app's private storage (a JSON file under the app's user:// directory). This file is never uploaded, and it is removed when you uninstall the app.

The “Copy the steps” button writes a plain-text job note to your device's clipboard only when you tap it. The app does not read the clipboard.

Permissions

The app requests only the VIBRATE permission, used for short haptic feedback when you tap controls. It does not require an internet connection to work, and it requests no location, camera, microphone, contacts, phone, SMS, or file-storage permissions.

Children

EMT Bender Buddy is a trade tool intended for electricians and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app's practices ever change, we will update this page and the app's store listing before the change takes effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to DumpsterWare@gmail.com.

EMT Bender Buddy is not affiliated with any bender manufacturer.
